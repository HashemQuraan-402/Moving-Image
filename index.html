Parallax

About the Project

This front-end experiment builds depth from independent visual layers. Scrolling moves the stars, moon, mountains, river, boat, and title at different speeds to produce a parallax effect.

Interaction

The scene reacts continuously to the page position. The title grows within a controlled range, the landscape layers shift independently, and the background changes as the visitor moves beyond the hero section.

Implementation

The page uses semantic HTML, responsive CSS, and dependency-free JavaScript. Animation updates are scheduled with requestAnimationFrame to keep scroll handling smooth.
